Saturday tech support at church

When I started going to North Bay Korean Church in 9th grade, I was the only young person there. Which meant that every Saturday, I was also the tech support.

"Vera, how do I get Netflix?" "Vera, my email is locked again." "Vera, is this message a scam?" (It usually was.) YouTube, online banking, all of it.

From questions to a program

At first I just answered whatever came up. Then I started doing drop-in sessions in the church fellowship hall so people could come with their questions. After a while I noticed the same problems kept coming back, so I wrote an actual curriculum.

That became Techwise Seniors, a monthly volunteer program that I started through Key Club. Student volunteers come help and earn service hours, and it has grown beyond just our church now.

Not Techwise, but this is from GIRL Camp, the other place I teach. Teaching is teaching!

Why I care about this

Part of it is my grandma in Korea. She's 85 and loves bragging that she's the only one in her book club who knows how to use emojis. Older people want to learn this stuff, they just need someone patient to show them.

Honestly, teaching taught me more than I expected. If you can explain online safety to someone who has never used a smartphone, you can probably explain anything.
